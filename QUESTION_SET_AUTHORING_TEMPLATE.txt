--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Which layer of the OSI model does a network router operate on?
(A) Physical Layer
(B) Data Link Layer
(C) Network Layer
(D) Transport Layer
Answer: C
Explanation: Routers operate at Layer 3 (Network Layer) to direct IP packet traffic between distinct networks.

TYPE 3: ASSERTION AND REASON (உறுதிக்கூற்று - காரணம்)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Assertion and Reason:
Assertion (A): Light travels faster in vacuum than in glass.
Reason (R): The refractive index of vacuum is 1, which is less than the refractive index of glass.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer:
(A) Both (A) and (R) are correct, and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
(B) Both (A) and (R) are correct, but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A).
(C) (A) is correct, but (R) is incorrect.
(D) (A) is incorrect, but (R) is correct.
Answer: A
Explanation: The speed of light is inversely proportional to the refractive index of the medium.

4. Consider the following statements regarding the Reserve Bank of India (RBI):
(i) RBI was established on April 1, 1935 under the Reserve Bank of India Act.
(ii) The headquarters of RBI was permanently moved to Mumbai in 1937.
(iii) Sir Osborne Smith was the first Governor of RBI.
(iv) RBI was nationalized in the year 1969.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(A) (i), (ii) and (iii) only
(B) (i) and (iv) only
(C) (ii), (iii) and (iv) only
(D) (i), (ii), (iii) and (iv)
Answer: A
Explanation: Statement (iv) is incorrect because RBI was nationalized on January 1, 1949 (not 1969).

TYPE 5: SEQUENCE / CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER (காலவரிசை வினா)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Arrange the following computer innovations in chronological order:
(i) Invention of the Transistor at Bell Labs
(ii) Launch of the World Wide Web (WWW)
(iii) Development of the C Programming Language
(iv) Invention of the Microprocessor (Intel 4004)

Choose the correct chronological sequence:
(A) (i) -> (iv) -> (iii) -> (ii)
(B) (i) -> (iii) -> (iv) -> (ii)
(C) (iv) -> (i) -> (iii) -> (ii)
(D) (ii) -> (i) -> (iii) -> (iv)
Answer: A
Explanation: Transistor (1947) -> Microprocessor (1971) -> C Language (1972) -> World Wide Web (1989).

Directions: Read the following passage carefully and answer Questions 6 and 7:
Passage:
Photosynthesis is the biological process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose molecules. This reaction takes place inside chloroplasts using chlorophyll. Water molecules are split in the light reactions to release oxygen as a byproduct.

6. In which cell organelle does photosynthesis primarily take place?
(A) Mitochondria
(B) Chloroplast
(C) Ribosome
(D) Endoplasmic Reticulum
Answer: B
Explanation: Photosynthesis takes place inside chloroplasts.

7. What is released as a byproduct during the light reactions of photosynthesis?
(A) Nitrogen
(B) Oxygen
(C) Carbon dioxide
(D) Hydrogen
Answer: B
Explanation: Photolysis of water releases oxygen (O2).

TYPE 7: MATHEMATICAL PROBLEM SOLVING & DATA INTERPRETATION (அட்டவணை வினாக்கள்)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. The following table shows the production of cars (in thousands) by four companies from 2021 to 2023:
Company | 2021 | 2022 | 2023
Alpha | 40 | 55 | 70
Beta | 30 | 45 | 60
Gamma | 50 | 60 | 65
Delta | 20 | 35 | 50

What is the percentage increase in the production of cars by Company Alpha from 2021 to 2023?
(A) 50%
(B) 60%
(C) 75%
(D) 80%
Answer: C
Explanation: Production in 2021 = 40. Production in 2023 = 70.
Increase = 70 - 40 = 30.
Percentage Increase = (30 / 40) * 100 = 75%.

CRITICAL AUTHORING RULES TO PREVENT ANY STRUCTURAL COLLAPSE:
================================================================================
1. Every question MUST start with a number on a new line:
   Valid: 1. or Q1: or வினா 1: or 90. பல்கூற்றுத் தேர்வு வினா
2. Never break category titles across lines (keep "பல்கூற்றுத் தேர்வு வினா" together).
3. In Statement questions, label items with (i), (ii), (iii), (iv).
4. In Match questions, use "List I" and "List II" (or "பட்டியல் I" and "பட்டியல் II").
5. Always provide 4 distinct options labeled (A)-(D) or (1)-(4) or (அ)-(ஈ).
6. Always end with the answer key: Answer: C or சரியான விடை: A or பதில்: (ஆ).
