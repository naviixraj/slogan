1. Which layer of the OSI model does a network router operate on?
(A) Physical Layer
(B) Data Link Layer
(C) Network Layer
(D) Transport Layer

List I (Protocols)
(A) HTTP

(D) DNS

3. Assertion and Reason:
Assertion (A): Light travels faster in vacuum than in glass.
Reason (R): The refractive index of vacuum is 1, which is less than the refractive index of glass.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer:
(A) Both (A) and (R) are correct, and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
(B) Both (A) and (R) are correct, but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A).
(C) (A) is correct, but (R) is incorrect.
(D) (A) is incorrect, but (R) is correct.
Answer: A
Explanation: Velocity of light v = c / n. Since refractive index of vacuum is 1, light travels at its maximum speed.

5. Consider the following statements regarding the Indian Constitution:
(i) The original Constitution contained 395 Articles and 8 Schedules.
(ii) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.
(iii) The Constitution was adopted on January 26, 1950.
(iv) The Preamble is an integral part of the Constitution as per the Kesavananda Bharati case.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(A) (i), (ii) and (iv) only
(B) (i) and (iii) only
(C) (ii), (iii) and (iv) only
(D) (i), (ii), (iii) and (iv)
Answer: A
Explanation: Statement (iii) is incorrect because the Constitution was adopted on November 26, 1949 and came into effect on January 26, 1950.

TYPE 5: SEQUENCE / CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER (காலவரிசை வினா)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Arrange the following computer innovations in chronological order:
(i) Invention of the Transistor
(ii) Release of the World Wide Web
(iii) Development of the C Programming Language
(iv) Invention of the Microprocessor (Intel 4004)

Choose the correct chronological sequence:
(A) (i) -> (iv) -> (iii) -> (ii)
(B) (i) -> (iii) -> (iv) -> (ii)
(C) (iv) -> (i) -> (iii) -> (ii)
(D) (ii) -> (i) -> (iii) -> (iv)
Answer: A
Explanation: Transistor (1947) -> Intel 4004 (1971) -> C Language (1972) -> World Wide Web (1989).

TYPE 7: MATHEMATICAL PROBLEM SOLVING & DATA INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. The following table shows the number of students enrolled in three courses from 2021 to 2023:
Course | 2021 | 2022 | 2023
Physics | 120 | 150 | 180
Chemistry | 80 | 100 | 140
Mathematics | 100 | 120 | 160

What is the percentage increase in total student enrollment from 2021 to 2023?
(A) 40%
(B) 50%
(C) 60%
(D) 70%
Answer: C
Explanation: Total in 2021 = 120 + 80 + 100 = 300.
Total in 2023 = 180 + 140 + 160 = 480.
Increase = 480 - 300 = 180.
Percentage Increase = (180 / 300) * 100 = 60%.
