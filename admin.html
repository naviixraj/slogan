Master Administrator Help

How do I add a new Examination Set?
Click the + Upload Set button in the toolbar. Enter the exam title as you want candidates to see it, select an Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or JSON file, and click submit. The system will automatically parse and split every row into separate questions.

What is the required Excel format?
Use standard 7 columns: Question, Option A, Option B, Option C, Option D, Answer, and Explanation. Bilingual questions (English and Tamil) in the same cell are fully supported.

How are candidate logins managed?
Go to the Students tab from the sidebar. You can enroll candidates by creating their unique User ID and password. Passwords are saved with BCrypt encryption.
