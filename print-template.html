Authoring Guidelines: Do's & Don'ts Checklist Standard rules to ensure 100% parsing accuracy
Category DO ✓ Recommended DON'T ✗ Avoid
Question Numbers Start every question with 1., 2., Q1:, or வினா 1: on a fresh line. Don't blend questions together in one continuous paragraph without a number.
Prompt Badges Keep பல்கூற்றுத் தேர்வு வினா or காலவரிசை வினா intact on its own line. Don't split titles like பல்கூற்று and த் தேர்வு வினா across two separate lines.
Statements & Lists Label statements clearly with (i), (ii), (iii), (iv). Don't write statements as raw run-on text without numeral markers.
Answer Keys Always end each question with Answer: X, சரியான விடை: X, or பதில்: X. Don't omit the answer line or place it before the choices.
